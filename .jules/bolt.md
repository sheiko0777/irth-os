## 2026-05-26 - Parallelize Pagination Data and Count Queries\n**Learning:** In Drizzle ORM/Postgres workflows, sequential execution of `.select()...limit().offset()` and `.select({ count: count() })` queries for pagination results in unnecessary sequential database round-trips and increased latency.\n**Action:** Always wrap independent pagination data fetching and count queries using `Promise.all()` to execute them concurrently and improve latency.
