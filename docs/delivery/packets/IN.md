## Cut
- FIFO / per-lot cost layers or a separate valuation ledger — Synthesis decision 7 and astra both accept WAC; lots carry quantity and state only. Upgrade path: add cost_minor to stock_lots if the accountant requires actual-lot costing.
- products.stock and product_variants.stock integer columns — Three places for one number (with inventory_items.quantity). Stop writing them in S1, drop in S2 after the admin reads only positions. Schema at packages/db/src/schema.ts:96 and :131.
- Legacy env-var Shopify path: setShopifyInventoryQuantity in apps/api/src/services/shopify.ts and SHOPIFY_ORG_ID fallback in resolveWebhookOrg — Duplicate of the per-connection function; the connector kernel (CX) removes the global-credential path anyway.
- Reservation TTL / auto-expiry engine — Orders do not expire on their own; holds are manual actions. Add a cron only if abandoned reservations are observed.
- Lot-level reservations — FEFO at pick time is the standard; reserving specific lots at order time only matters for B2B min-shelf-life, which the allocation destination rule handles.
- Multi-level BOM explosion, routing/work centres, scheduling, planned scrap %, standard-cost variances (material/labour/overhead) — Astra F: cut scheduling optimisation and elaborate variance accounting; keep actual consumption/output/loss. Bulk→pack is two single-level BOMs.
- Separate 'WIP mode' toggle — Always posting through 1045 WIP is fewer branches and automatically values open batches at period end.
- Bins/shelf locations inside a warehouse, serial numbers, cycle-count scheduler, consignment, drop-ship — Single warehouse today; none of these are in the owner's confirmed facts.
- Generic UoM conversion engine and decimal stock quantities — Integer base units + 6 static conversions cover cosmetics bulk (g/ml) and packaging (ea).
- Offline POS, POS hardware/terminal drivers, barcode scanner app — Owner locked online-only web POS; mobile warehouse app is 'later' in the synthesis.
- Supplier RFQ, catalogs, scorecards, return-to-supplier workflow — RBAC research MVP excludes them; return-to-supplier deferred until a real supplier return happens.
- Landed cost and GRNI in S2 — Moved to S3 with supplier bills; S2 keeps the existing direct Dr Inventory Cr AP receipt posting.
- ERPNext as owner of physical stock (sol-tier recommendation) — Owner rejected Option C; ERPNext remains an accounting-close gate only (S4).
- Kits/bundles as order-time explosion — A pre-packed kit is a manufactured variant with a BOM; ad-hoc bundles are an OR-domain multi-line concern.
- inventory.adjust direct 'set'/'in'/'out' mutation without approval — Replaced by write-off/adjustment proposals through the approvals table (astra approval policy #2).

## Open questions
- Does legal title to stock pass to the brand entity before the customer sale (brand-owned positions, physical stock unchanged) or only at the accountant-approved recognition point together with the customer sale (parent ships, IC invoice posted then)? — why: Determines whether stock positions ever carry a brand entity as owner (a transfer-of-ownership move per shipment) or remain parent-owned with a ledger-only intercompany posting; affects IN-01 backfill, IN-05 shipOrderLines and the DM intercompany task. — default: Parent owns every position through S3; intercompany is ledger-only at recognition; no brand-owned positions are created.
- Manufacturing overhead policy: none, % of material cost, or fixed amount per batch — and who approves changes? — why: IN-12 output cost and per-brand margin; astra says missing costs must be labelled, not zeroed. — default: 'none'; margin reports show 'overhead: not allocated'.
- Which variants are received into quarantine pending QC: all lot-tracked items, only raw ingredients + manufactured outputs, or nothing (release on receipt)? — why: IN-09 initial_state default and how much stock is invisible to ATP until someone clicks release. — default: Raw ingredients and manufactured outputs → quarantined; resale finished goods and packaging → released on receipt (per-variant receive_into_state, owner editable).
- Minimum remaining shelf life for D2C storefront/POS shipments and for B2B customers (days)? — why: IN-05 hard block threshold and IN-25 B2B rule; too high strands sellable stock, too low ships near-expiry goods. — default: D2C/POS 60 days; B2B default 180 days per customer, overridable.
- Write-off / adjustment approval: single approver (owner) for everything, or a value threshold below which the warehouse lead self-approves? — why: IN-14 policy configuration and daily friction for small expiry write-offs. — default: Owner approves every write-off/adjustment; no self-approval threshold.
- POS tenders: cash, card via a bank terminal (manual reference entry), InstaPay — any terminal integration expected, and what cash-variance threshold needs owner approval at till close? — why: IN-16 tender model and approval trigger. — default: Manual tender entry with reference; variance > 100 EGP requires approval.
- Are integer base-unit quantities (grams, millilitres, each) sufficient for all ingredients, or are there sub-gram actives that need decimal quantities? — why: IN-10 uom model; switching to numeric(18,3) later touches every quantity column. — default: Integer base units.
- Do the three brands' storefronts sell from one shared parent stock pool with per-channel buffers, or does each brand get a fixed allocation of units? — why: IN-06 policy model (buffer only vs allocation quotas); quotas need a third table and reconciliation of unallocated stock. — default: Shared pool with per-channel buffer only.
- Can the owner provide an opening-stock CSV with lot numbers and expiry dates per SKU from the paper records, or only total quantities? — why: IN-03 fixture; without lots the S1 FEFO/recall path is exercised on synthetic lots only. — default: One 'OPENING' lot per SKU with expiry NULL, flagged expiry_unknown for later correction.
- Accountant preference for receipt posting: GRNI (Dr Inventory Cr GRNI at receipt, Dr GRNI Cr AP at bill) or direct Dr Inventory Cr AP at receipt as today? — why: IN-19 size and IN-09 posting; ERPNext fit-test (S4) will ask the same question. — default: Direct AP in S2 (unchanged); GRNI introduced with supplier bills in S3.
- WooCommerce: is one Woo store always linked to exactly one warehouse (no location concept in WC REST)? — why: IN-18 publish target and IN-23 channel_warehouse_links for Woo. — default: Yes, one warehouse per Woo store; multi-warehouse ATP is summed before publishing.
