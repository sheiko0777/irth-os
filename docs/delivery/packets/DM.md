## Cut
- parties table + customers/suppliers/employees as role tables (synthesis decision #6) — now — customers is already group-level (org == group); rename is churn without S1 payoff. Add when one person genuinely plays two roles.
- account_balances incremental rollup maintained in the posting transaction — SMB volume: SUM(journal_lines) over (entity, account, date) indexes serves TB/P&L for years. Add when a report exceeds ~2s.
- Hash-chained, partitioned audit_log with unlimited denial logging — Astra: optional; grant-based append-only + tested restore is the real integrity control.
- cost_center dimension / dimension_rules table — Brand is the only cost centre the business has; a 6-line required-dimension map in ledger.ts covers 'brand+channel required on revenue/COGS'.
- FIFO / valuation-layer ledger (ERP research proposal) — WAC + lot_id on moves is what the owner's costing needs and what costing.ts already does correctly.
- GUC-array entity/brand/warehouse scoping for staff (app.entity_ids etc.) in S0 — One ops team, one accountant across all entities — no staff principal needs narrowing before the supplier portal (S3).
- One organization per brand / Better Auth organization plugin — Forces 4 memberships per shared employee and makes consolidated reads illegitimate under RLS (synthesis #1, #11).
- Squashing 66 migrations into a fresh baseline — Invalidates the _migrations ledger on every branch for zero functional gain; additive files with DROP/recreate achieve the same clean shape.
- journals lookup table, tax_codes/tax_rates tables in S0/S1 — journal_type enum suffices (0038 rationale); Egyptian flat 14% VAT via EGYPT_VAT_BP is correct for S1's Egyptian entities; per-entity VAT regimes are an S4 (ETA/EU) task.
- Brand-side stock positions in S1 — Flash title at recognition point means brands never hold stock; adding positions for them is speculative until the accountant asks for brand-held inventory.
- Stored translated (presentation-currency) ledger — Translation is a report-time calculation over functional amounts + exchange_rates (IAS 21); storing it creates a second book to reconcile.
- apps/mobile, platform-admin layer, org_feature_flags, packages/types — Locked deletions from Option B; DM only drops org_feature_flags + activity_log tables in its migrations.

## Open questions
- Which legal entities exist exactly (names, countries, functional currency each), and is the Spanish/EUR entity a seller of stock or only a sales entity? Which entity is the stock owner (assumed IRTH parent)? — why: DM-01 seed, DM-09 transfer prices, DM-21 translation and the S4 VAT regimes all key on this; a Gulf entity vs Gulf-currency sales from the Egyptian entity changes tax jurisdiction (astra: currency alone does not determine tax jurisdiction). — default: 4 entities: IRTH parent (EG, EGP, stock owner) + 3 brand entities (EG, EGP); EUR/SAR/AED handled as presentment currencies on channels, not as separate entities, until the accountant confirms otherwise.
- Revenue recognition point per channel: COD at delivery (today's behaviour), online at payment capture or at shipment, B2B at invoice? — why: DM-10 posts only at the configured point; the intercompany invoice date follows it and ETA document timing (S4) depends on it. — default: delivered for COD and online storefront orders; invoiced for B2B; paid for POS. Stored per entity with channel override (DM-09).
- Transfer price method between parent and brands (cost-plus % or fixed price list) and whether brands take title before sale (brand-held stock) or at the instant of customer sale (flash title). — why: Flash title removes brand stock positions and makes unrealized IC profit elimination zero; brand-held stock requires transfer moves, brand positions and a real elimination engine (DM-21 grows). — default: cost-plus 20% wildcard row in the seed; flash title at recognition point.
- Should the single accountant be entity-restricted at any point, or is one accountant with full-group access the permanent model? — why: Decides whether app.entity_ids scoping (cut from S0) ever needs to exist; if yes it is a DM-18-style additive predicate, not a redesign. — default: Full-group accountant; entity scoping only for suppliers (DM-18).
- Do batches genuinely complete within a day, or does bulk product sit overnight/across month-end (needs the simple WIP state and close check)? — why: DM-16 ships a 'wip' status and DM-20 blocks soft close on consumed-but-unfinished value; if batches always complete same day this is dead weight. — default: Keep the wip state; it is one enum value and one close check.
- Are the existing production Neon DB rows (one org, test orders) to be wiped before S1, or must migrations preserve them? — why: Migrations are written to backfill either way, but wiping lets DM-05/DM-07 skip orphan-row handling for orders with dropped items. — default: Preserve via backfill (the safer path); the owner may truncate the app DB after S1 acceptance.
- Minimum remaining shelf life per destination/channel (e.g. marketplaces require ≥6 months) — a real number or not applicable yet? — why: DM-08 allocateLots takes minShelfLifeDays from the channel; without a policy FEFO alone may ship near-expiry stock to B2B. — default: 0 days on all channels; column exists so the number can be set later.
